Number of cosmetics per hero per type
Notes:
Does not include default items (add +1 to each number to account for that)
Number in brackets are related units that are shown in a preview. Otherwise those units are discarded (Beastmaster's boar sets are counted, Enigma's eidolons are not)
Cosmetics affecting other forms are not included (Lone druid's True Form, Terrorblade's Metamorphosis, Lycan's Shapeshift)
Taunts, Herro effigies, "Costumes" are not counted
Unusual variations of items with special effects are not counted
Does not account for different styles of the same item
Legion Commander has conflicting weapons with her arcana, so her arcana hero base is not counted
Tiny's "Medium" and "Large" slots are not counted
